Samples

Every uploaded file becomes a sample. Conversion and ingestion run in a queue with a single worker - in parallel they would fight over ClickHouse memory, which answers that with an error rather than with slowness.

Upload files

On automatic, a Thermo .raw goes to ThermoRawFileParser with -x, and everything else to msconvert. The -x flag excludes the exception data (lock mass, AGC overflow, internal calibration) that msconvert keeps in the mzML - it is not analyte, and it turns into false positives in the pipeline.
